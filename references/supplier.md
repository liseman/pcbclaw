# Authorized quotation, cart and engineering review

1. Establish scope: selected release hashes, assembled finished-board quantity, assembly service, allowed uploads, cart edits, communications and payment authority. Existing authorization persists; do not request it again. Normal design work does not authorize uploads, messages, purchases or payment. A cart request authorizes its preparation, not checkout. Follow the installed browser workflow and preserve unrelated tabs/items.
2. Check layer count, detected dimensions, finish, actual stackup, rails/panels, boards per panel and assembled versus fabricated quantities after upload. Upload fabrication ZIP, BOM and CPL from the same validated revision, never the entire delivery archive into the Gerber field.
3. Inspect every matched part's exact MPN/LCSC, selected/fitted state, required spares, stock and price. A matched row may still be unchecked (including connectors); confirm every intended part is selected. Never silently substitute. Stock equal to fitted quantity has no attrition margin unless the live quote accepts that requirement. Public-stock lookup does not require sign-in; continue independent public/local work while login is unavailable.
4. Compare exported placements to source geometry and then inspect actual supplier models/marked previews for pin 1, cathode, orientation and alignment. A reference-set match is not a supplier-model check. Empty images/placeholders do not support sign-off. Preserve manual production-file and placement approval when unresolved, where the supplier supports it; verify the saved setting.
5. Check exact-part soldering evidence: distinguish recommended process, maximum heat-resistance envelope, minimum process requirement, package temperature, MSL and exposure duration. Do not turn a maximum survival profile into a required minimum peak. Review stencil, thermal vias, mask webs, slots/connector fit and actual stackup; request supplier engineering disposition for unresolved process questions. Cart remarks are requests, not acceptance.
6. If the user authorizes contacting the supplier, send a bounded review packet with exact revisions/files and questions. Avoid unrelated private data. Verify attachment/submission results; distinguish form success from ticket receipt and engineering approval. Record responses and any unresolved issue. Check whether the supplier's review occurs before or after order/payment; do not invent a prepayment approval path or promise its response time.
7. Save the live itemized quote with currency/time, fabricated/assembled/panel counts, inclusion/exclusion list and stock limitations. Identify partial subtotals separately. Do not call uploaded, quoted or cart-saved items production-ready.
8. When replacing cart items, add and verify revised entries before deleting superseded entries. Delete unrelated items only when explicitly authorized. Re-read final cart to verify project revision, quantity, service, prices and retained review conditions; save evidence without credentials. Stop before checkout/payment unless separately authorized.
